FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What fabrics do you use?

Premium fabrics including raw silk, velvet, georgette, and handwoven textiles from master weavers across India.

Can I customise a design?

Yes! All designs can be customised in colour, fabric, and size.

Do you ship across India?

Yes, we ship across India. Standard delivery takes 3–7 business days depending on your location.

Are embroideries hand-done?

Yes — all Zari, Cutdana, and Sequin work is done by hand by skilled artisans.

How do I care for my garment?

Dry clean only for blazers and bandhgalas. Hand wash cold for shirts.

Our Story

About YANARA Fashion

Born from a passion for Indian craft and contemporary design, YANARA Fashion is where tradition meets elegance.

Our Mission

Celebrating Indian Craft

YANARA Fashion was founded with a single vision — to bring the finest Indian ethnic wear to modern men who value craftsmanship, heritage, and style.

Every piece is handcrafted by skilled artisans using age-old techniques — Zari embroidery, Cutdana work, hand painting, and more.

The Designer

Designer Ramya

Award-winning fashion designer with over a decade of experience. From Big Boss Tamil to London concert stages — Ramya's work has graced screens, runways, and red carpets across India and abroad.
